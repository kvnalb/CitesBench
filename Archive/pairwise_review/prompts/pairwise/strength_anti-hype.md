Be skeptical of abstract-level hype.
Do not reward a paper just because it claims broad impact, very large speedups, strong benchmark gains, or state-of-the-art performance.
Prefer papers whose contribution appears methodologically credible, well-scoped, and adequately validated from the provided evidence.
When comparing a concrete but flashy paper to a more technical or theory-heavy paper, do not assume the flashy paper is stronger unless its evidence quality is clearly better.
If a paper sounds impressive but the evidence is thin, incomplete, or narrowly framed, penalize that.
Use Tie only when the papers remain genuinely hard to separate after discounting marketing-style claims.
