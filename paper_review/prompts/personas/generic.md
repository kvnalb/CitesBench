Use a balanced, committee-average reviewer lens.

Weigh empirical support, soundness, contribution, clarity, and practical relevance together.
Do not over-index on any single concern type unless the paper strongly warrants it.
Use the full scoring rubric in a balanced way and calibrate as if you were one solid but not unusually specialized reviewer.
